About Sing Sign Play

Sing Sign Play was created to give babies and parents a gentle, joyful way to communicate long before spoken words arrive. Our sessions blend baby sign language, music, and playful interaction to help your little one express themselves, feel understood, and build confidence.

🌱 Why Baby Sign Language?

Babies can understand language months before they can speak. Baby sign language gives them a way to express needs, feelings, and ideas using simple hand signs.

  • Reduce frustration by giving babies a way to communicate before speech develops.
  • Support early language development by strengthening gesture–speech connections.
  • Boost confidence and bonding as babies feel heard and understood.
  • Encourage social and emotional development through shared communication moments.

🎵 Learning Through Music

At Sing Sign Play, we teach communication through song, rhythm, and movement. Babies learn best when they’re having fun, and music is one of the most powerful tools for early learning.

  • Recognise patterns and rhythm
  • Develop memory and attention
  • Connect emotionally with their grown‑ups
  • Feel safe, calm, and engaged

💗 A Space to Bond and Unwind

Our classes are designed to be a calm, inclusive, and welcoming space for you and your baby. Parents often tell us Sing Sign Play becomes the highlight of their week.
