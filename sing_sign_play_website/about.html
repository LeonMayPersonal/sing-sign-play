About Sing Sign Play

Sing Sign Play was created to give babies and parents a gentle, joyful way to communicate long before spoken words arrive. Our sessions blend baby sign language, music, and playful interaction to help your little one express themselves, feel understood, and build confidence.

🌱 Why Baby Sign Language?

Babies can understand language months before they can speak. Baby sign language gives them a way to express needs, feelings, and ideas using simple hand signs. Research shows that early signing can:

  • Reduce frustration by giving babies a way to communicate before speech develops.
  • Support early language development by strengthening the connection between gesture, understanding, and spoken words.
  • Boost confidence and bonding as babies feel heard and understood.
  • Encourage social and emotional development through shared communication moments.

Studies from child development researchers (including work published in journals like *Child Development* and *Early Childhood Research Quarterly*) highlight that gesture and early symbolic communication help babies build stronger language pathways. Signing doesn’t replace speech — it supports it.

🎵 Learning Through Music

At Sing Sign Play, we teach communication through song, rhythm, and movement. Babies learn best when they’re having fun, and music is one of the most powerful tools for early learning. Singing helps babies:

  • Recognise patterns and rhythm
  • Develop memory and attention
  • Connect emotionally with their grown‑ups
  • Feel safe, calm, and engaged

Every session includes gentle songs, playful signing, sensory elements, and lots of smiles. You don’t need to be a singer — your baby loves your voice exactly as it is.

💗 A Space to Bond and Unwind

Our classes are designed to be a calm, inclusive, and welcoming space for you and your baby. It’s a chance to slow down, connect, and enjoy meaningful moments together. Parents often tell us that Sing Sign Play becomes the highlight of their week — a place where they can relax, meet other families, and watch their babies thrive.

Whether your baby is just beginning to explore the world or already full of personality, Sing Sign Play gives them the tools to communicate, express themselves, and shine.
